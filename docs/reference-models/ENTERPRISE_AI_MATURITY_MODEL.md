# Enterprise AI Maturity Model

> A reference model for assessing organizational AI maturity.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Maturity Model provides a structured framework for evaluating an organization's ability to design, deploy, govern and continuously evolve AI systems.

It focuses on engineering capabilities rather than model sophistication.

---

# Level 1 — Experimentation

Characteristics

- Isolated AI initiatives
- Individual experimentation
- No governance
- No production standards

Primary Risk

AI remains a collection of disconnected prototypes.

---

# Level 2 — Operationalization

Characteristics

- First production deployments
- Initial governance
- Basic monitoring
- Reusable prompts and workflows

Primary Risk

Scaling becomes difficult without architectural consistency.

---

# Level 3 — Platform

Characteristics

- Shared AI platform
- Standard deployment patterns
- Evaluation pipelines
- Centralized governance

Primary Risk

Operational complexity increases.

---

# Level 4 — Enterprise

Characteristics

- AI integrated across business domains
- Cross-functional governance
- Enterprise observability
- Security by design
- Continuous evaluation

Primary Risk

Managing organizational change.

---

# Level 5 — Adaptive Enterprise

Characteristics

- AI becomes an enterprise capability
- Continuous optimization
- Architecture evolves with the business
- Governance becomes data-driven

Primary Outcome

AI is managed as strategic infrastructure rather than isolated projects.
