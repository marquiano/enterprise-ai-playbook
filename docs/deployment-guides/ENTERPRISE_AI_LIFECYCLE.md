# Enterprise AI Lifecycle

> Canonical lifecycle for Enterprise AI initiatives.

---

# Overview

Successful Enterprise AI systems are not built around models.

They are built around an engineering lifecycle that continuously aligns business goals, architecture, deployment and operations.

---

# Phase 1 — Business Strategy

Identify business objectives, stakeholders and measurable outcomes.

Deliverable:

- Business Problem Definition

---

# Phase 2 — Use Case Discovery

Identify valuable AI opportunities.

Deliverables:

- Use Case Assessment
- Risk Assessment
- Feasibility Analysis

---

# Phase 3 — Architecture

Design the target architecture.

Deliverables:

- Reference Architecture
- Integration Strategy
- Security Model

---

# Phase 4 — Technology Decisions

Select technologies.

Deliverables:

- Engineering Decision Records
- Platform Selection
- Model Selection

---

# Phase 5 — Implementation

Build production-ready capabilities.

Deliverables:

- Services
- APIs
- Workflows
- Evaluation Pipelines

---

# Phase 6 — Evaluation

Measure quality continuously.

Deliverables:

- Benchmarks
- Acceptance Criteria
- Regression Tests

---

# Phase 7 — Deployment

Release safely.

Deliverables:

- CI/CD
- Rollout Strategy
- Monitoring

---

# Phase 8 — Operations

Operate AI systems reliably.

Deliverables:

- Observability
- Incident Management
- Governance Reviews

---

# Phase 9 — Continuous Improvement

Continuously evolve the platform.

Deliverables:

- Architecture Reviews
- Maturity Assessments
- Platform Evolution
