# Enterprise AI Principles

> Canonical engineering principles for designing, deploying, governing and operating Enterprise AI Systems.

---

# Purpose

These principles define the engineering philosophy adopted throughout the Enterprise AI Playbook.

They are technology-agnostic and intended to guide architectural decisions, implementation choices and operational practices.

---

# Principle 1 — Business Before Models

AI exists to solve business problems.

Model selection is an implementation detail.

---

# Principle 2 — Architecture Before Prompts

System architecture has a greater long-term impact than prompt engineering.

Architecture defines reliability, scalability and maintainability.

---

# Principle 3 — Governance by Design

Governance is not an additional layer.

Governance is part of the architecture itself.

---

# Principle 4 — Humans Own Decisions

AI may recommend.

Humans remain accountable.

---

# Principle 5 — Production First

Design systems for production from day one.

Avoid prototype-first architectures that cannot evolve.

---

# Principle 6 — Evaluation is Continuous

Every AI system requires measurable quality.

Evaluation is a continuous engineering process.

---

# Principle 7 — Security by Default

Security must be embedded into every architectural layer.

---

# Principle 8 — Observability Everywhere

Production AI requires visibility.

Systems that cannot be observed cannot be governed.

---

# Principle 9 — Simplicity at Scale

Prefer simple architectures that can evolve over complex architectures that impress.

---

# Principle 10 — Continuous Evolution

Enterprise AI systems are never finished.

They continuously evolve as business, models and regulations change.
